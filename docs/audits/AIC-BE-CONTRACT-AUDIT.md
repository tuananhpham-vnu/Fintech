### Domain invariants traced to source

SME = **organization** and authenticated specialist's tenant; **counterparty** = legal entity being evaluated. **RECEIVABLE**: SME seller, counterparty buyer; **PREPAYMENT**: SME buyer, counterparty seller. LOAN (lender/borrower) and DEBT_INVESTMENT (investor/issuer) are secondary. Cross-border counterparty supported. Exposure amount_vnd is a positive integer **string**; tax IDs remain strings with leading zeros. Financial statement amounts retain their own source currency/unit, accounting period and basis. A missing value, waived requirement and zero each have different semantics (01/02/10).

Lifecycle axes are independent: document processing; extraction quality/missing reason; verified vs SME_direct/staff_transcribed origin; finding state vs handling state; request lifecycle/revision; review/assessment state and source freshness; method lifecycle. A waiver never implies resolved/verified. A new document/correction/revision makes dependent published results **stale**, but old signed snapshots and approved export bytes persist (01/02/08/09/10). Human actors and server-assigned policy/organization/revisions must never come from AI.
